# 💫 About Me:
I am a Mechatronics Engineering student focused on biomedical instrumentation, embedded systems, and digital signal processing, building at the intersection of low-level firmware and clinical sensor technology.<br>Currently working on: Phonoangiography digital signal processing (DSP)—developing filtering and spectral analysis pipelines for acoustic vascular bruits to identify arterial stenosis non-invasively.<br><br>Looking to collaborate on: Open-source biomedical DSP algorithms, embedded health tech prototypes, and sensor fusion applications.<br><br>Ask me about: Microcontroller development (STM32, ESP32), sensor interfacing, circuit prototyping, and 3D printing/CAD for hardware enclosures.<br><br>Currently learning: Advanced discrete-time signal processing, wavelet transforms for non-stationary bioacoustics, and bare-metal ARM architecture optimization.<br><br>Looking for help with: Noise reduction strategies against ambient and physiological motion artifacts, as well as optimizing signal processing code for real-time edge microcontrollers.<br><br>Fun fact: Outside of engineering, I play acoustic drums and spend my spare time breaking down progressive metal odd-time signatures and polyrhythms.
